# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project should be a straightforward, welcoming place to contribute, for people of any
background, experience level or identity, and whichever variety of Belarusian (or other language)
they speak.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Settle disagreements about wording, spelling or orthography with sources, not with insults.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change.
- Respect the privacy of contributors and reviewers (reviewers are recorded by handle only).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Ethnic, national, religious or political baiting, in issues, reviews or proposed content.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Posting secrets, private keys, site secrets or real credentials in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately through GitHub (a private security
advisory on this repository works for anything sensitive), or in a public issue if it is not.
Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban a contributor depending on severity.
